# Privacy Policy

**EQ Legends BiS** is a desktop app for EverQuest Legends, by PolarOppositesHub.

**Effective date:** October 11, 2026

## No servers

The app has no backend. It collects nothing about you. There is no account with us, and nothing you do in the app is sent to PolarOppositesHub.

## Data stays on your PCs

Settings, characters, parsed game logs, inventory imports, and currency history are stored locally on your computer. That data stays on the PCs where you use the app.

## Optional sync

Sync is off unless you turn it on.

If you sign in, the app stores its sync files only in:

- Your own Google Drive hidden app-data folder, using the scope `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata`, and/or
- Your own OneDrive App Folder, using Microsoft Graph `https://graph.microsoft.com/Files.ReadWrite.AppFolder` and `offline_access`.

It cannot see or access any other files in your Drive or OneDrive.

You can instead pick a local sync folder, for example a folder in iCloud Drive or Dropbox. That folder is handled by that service's own client. The app only reads and writes the files in the folder you choose.

## Game files

The app reads EverQuest Legends log and export files on your PC only to show stats. Nothing from those files is uploaded, except to your own sync storage if you enable it.

## No sale, sharing, ads, or analytics

No data is sold, shared, or used for ads or analytics.

## Sign-in tokens

Sign-in tokens are stored only on your PC, encrypted with the operating system (Windows DPAPI via Electron safeStorage). Sign out removes them. You can revoke access any time in your Google or Microsoft account settings.

## Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Deleting your data

Delete the app's local data folder to remove what is stored on your PC.

To remove synced copies, delete the app's hidden data in Google Drive (Manage apps) or the OneDrive Apps folder.
